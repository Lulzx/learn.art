# v0.13 — Deterministic mini-batches

v0.13 adds paired row batching and the final state needed to resume a training session exactly.

## Added

- `batcher.size:.shuffle:.seed:` for paired feature and target tensors.
- Deterministic Fisher–Yates permutations from a local epoch-derived generator.
- `nextBatch` with owned tensors, source indices, and epoch metadata.
- `batcherState` and `loadBatcherState` for exact permutation/cursor restoration.
- `saveSessionCheckpoint` and `loadSessionCheckpoint` combining model, optimizer, and batcher state.
- Atomic validation across all three session components.

## Semantics

The final partial batch is returned without padding. The epoch advances on the call after the previous epoch is exhausted, at which point a deterministic new permutation is installed. Checkpoints taken at the end boundary resume with that same transition.

## Boundary

v0.13 provides iteration and state, not a policy-heavy training loop. Validation datasets, callbacks, early stopping, and loss-history collection remain the next layer.
